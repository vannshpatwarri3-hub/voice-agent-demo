Live demo

AI voice agent

A Hinglish-speaking agent that answers pre-calls, works out who is calling and what they need, and then hands off. Click Talk to Atoms in the bottom-right corner and speak to it.

How it opens

"Namaste. Main Vannsh Patwarri ka personal assistent hun. Kya main jaan sakta hun ki aap kon baat kar rahain hai?" "Hello. I'm Vannsh Patwarri's personal assistant. May I know who I'm speaking with?"

Try it

  1. Click Talk to Atoms, bottom-right
  2. Press Start and allow microphone access when prompted
  3. Just talk — say who you are and what you're calling about
